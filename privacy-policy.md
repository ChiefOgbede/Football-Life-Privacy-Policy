# Football Life — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 23, 2026

Football Life (“the App”) is a football quiz app. This policy explains what information the App may handle and how it is used.

## Information the App handles

- **Quiz answers, scores and progress:** These are used to run quizzes and show your results. If the App stores this information only on your device, it stays there unless you choose to share it. If online accounts, cloud saving or leaderboards are added, this policy must be updated to explain that.
- **Information collected by third-party services:** If the App uses advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or similar services (for example, Google AdMob or Firebase), those providers may collect device identifiers, app activity, diagnostic information and other data as described in their own privacy policies.
- **Information you send us:** If you contact the developer, the information in your message and contact details may be used to respond.

## How information is used

Information is used to operate the quizzes, remember progress where supported, improve app performance, understand technical issues and, if enabled, display advertising.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the App, such as advertising, analytics or crash-reporting providers, if those services are integrated. Those providers handle information under their own policies.

## Children's privacy

The App is not intended to collect personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer so the issue can be reviewed.

## Data security and retention

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information handled by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information is kept only for as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. Data stored by third-party providers is subject to their retention practices.

## Your choices

You can stop using the App at any time. You can manage device permissions and advertising preferences through your device settings, where those controls are available. You may also contact the developer with privacy questions or requests.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
