# Football Life — User Data Deletion Instructions

**Effective date:** November 23, 2026

If you want to request deletion of personal information associated with Football Life, follow these steps:

1. Send an email to **[Add your contact email]**.
2. Use the subject line **“Football Life — Data Deletion Request”**.
3. In your message, explain that you want your personal information deleted. Include only the information needed to help us identify the relevant data. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.
4. We will review your request and may contact you if we need reasonable information to verify it.
5. Once verified, we will delete personal information under our control unless we are legally required or permitted to retain it. We will let you know when the request has been handled.

## Data handled by third-party services

If the App uses services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting or cloud storage, those providers may process data under their own privacy policies. Some data may need to be deleted through the relevant provider's privacy or account controls.

## Data stored only on your device

If quiz progress or preferences are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers, uninstalling the App or clearing its app storage may remove that local data.
